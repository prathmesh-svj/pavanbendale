How much does IVF cost? Is EMI available?
+
Yes. IVF packages start at EMI of ₹9,999/month with completely transparent pricing — no hidden charges. Dr. Pavan believes cost should never be a barrier to parenthood.
What is your IVF success rate?
+
Our IVF success rates average 60–65% per cycle — above national benchmarks. Individual outcomes depend on age, diagnosis, and embryo quality, which Dr. Pavan discusses in detail during consultation.
When should I see a fertility specialist?
+
If you're under 35 and have been trying for 12 months without success, or over 35 and trying for 6 months — it's time to consult. Earlier evaluation always leads to better outcomes.
Can PCOS patients do IVF successfully?
+
Yes. PCOS patients often respond well to IVF with modified stimulation protocols. Dr. Pavan specializes in PCOS-related fertility challenges and has helped hundreds of PCOS patients conceive.
Do you treat male infertility as well?
+
Absolutely. 6Venus has a full urology department specializing in male infertility — including TESA, Micro-TESE sperm retrieval, and semen analysis — all under one roof.
